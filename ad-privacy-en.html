Ad Privacy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Accord shows ads via Google AdMob to stay free. Before showing you ads, we ask for your consent under GDPR.

1. Consent

If you accept, some ads may be personalized using data provided by Google. If you decline, you'll still see ads, just non-personalized ones.

2. Changing your mind

You can change your mind at any time by resetting your iPhone's tracking settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

3. Learn more

To learn more about Google's advertising policy, visit policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
